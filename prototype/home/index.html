03 How I work

Designers who can hold both ends, strategy and craft, are rare.

I move between product direction and production detail without losing the thread.

Lifecycle, not the screen I map the whole operating loop—before, during, and after the click—so the product solves the actual job.
Systems first, screens second I create reusable rules that help teams make consistent decisions without slowing down the work.
Density is not the enemy For expert tools, clarity comes from hierarchy and context—not from hiding the information people need.
