Impact map

Four orbits of design impact around one product, from a single screen to business results.

Interface: one screen
  • Experience: How the product feels as a whole: calm and predictable.
  • Clarity: At a glance it is clear what is going on and what to do next. Case: Channel Analysis.
  • Usability: A complex task gets done fast and without mistakes. Case: Booking Calendar (coming soon).
Flow: end to end
  • Impact: The solution changes how people work, not just a screen.
  • Confidence: Users trust the numbers and recommendations because they can see where they come from. Case: Price Exploration.
  • Self-service: Customers get it done on their own, without support or an account manager. Case: Inbox, Camilyo.
Team: how others work
  • Governance: It is clear who changes the system and how, so it does not sprawl.
  • Guidelines: Rules that let the team make the same calls without a designer.
  • Design System: The components and tokens every screen is built from. Case: Design System for AI Agents.
Business: what the numbers show
  • Adoption: Features get used, not just shipped.
  • Scale: The solution works for thousands of customers, not just one. Case: Cross-Campaign Management.
  • Outcomes: A measurable result: time, money, support tickets.

How I work

I design the whole loop, not just the screen.

Media buyers, small business owners, AI agents. Different users, same habit: find where the work really breaks, then fix it at the root.

BeforeScreenAfter
01

Follow the job, not the screen

A screen is one moment in someone's day. I start from the whole job: what triggers it, who else is involved, what happens after. Then I design the path, not the page.

Seen inInbox
02

Make the black box readable

People don't act on what they don't understand. When a system makes a call, I show why it did, and turn that into one clear next step.

Fix it once, for everyone

If a problem keeps coming back, I solve the pattern, not the instance. Rules, components and bulk actions that keep working as the product and the team grow.
