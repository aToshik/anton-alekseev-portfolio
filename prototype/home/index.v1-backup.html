04 · Why work with me

Designers who can hold both ends, strategy and craft, are rare.

From the shape of the system to the detail of the interaction.

I design from the lifecycle, not the screen.

I design around how people discover, use, return to, and grow with a product, building systems that feel connected and easy to trust.

Systems first, screens second.

I start with the structure behind the product, then shape the screens around it, so the experience stays consistent, scalable, and easier to use.

Density is not the enemy.

I don’t simplify by removing what matters. I organize dense information so people can scan, understand, and act with confidence.
